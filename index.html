How to play

The opening screen inside the game has a full guide. This is the short version.

1

Protect your core

Both players begin at 76 health. Reduce the opposing core to zero to win.

2

Build your board

Draw one card each turn. Your mana refills and grows by one. Play cards into five board slots.

3

Choose your targets

Specific Battlecries ask you to point at their victim. Effects that explicitly say random remain random.

4

Strike together

Combat is simultaneous. Defenders hit back even when the incoming attack destroys them.

5

Respect Taunt

A standing Taunt closes the core. Remove it before attacking past it.

6

Read the colours

Green means ready to attack, red marks legal victims, blue means a hand card can be played, and teal means selected.
